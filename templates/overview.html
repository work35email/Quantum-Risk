Classical simulation

Exact enumeration, Monte Carlo, scrambled Sobol, stratified sampling and importance sampling estimate the same contract.

Tail-aware proposals draw more often from rare years in which several countries are hit hard, then correct the estimates with likelihood ratios.

Tensor train / MPS

Tensor train / MPS is a separate experiment. A tensor network writes a large probability table as a chain of smaller arrays. Quimb contracts that chain. Cotengra chooses the order of the contraction.

Agreement with exact enumeration is a validity check, not a faster or quantum result.
